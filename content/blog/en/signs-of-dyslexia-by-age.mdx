Dyslexia manifests differently depending on age. The earlier it is detected, the more effective the help.

### At age 5

The child has difficulty learning letters, confuses sounds, cannot remember simple rhymes.

### At age 7

Reads significantly slower than peers, confuses similar letters, avoids reading.

### At age 10

Difficulties with text comprehension, slow reading, spelling problems.
