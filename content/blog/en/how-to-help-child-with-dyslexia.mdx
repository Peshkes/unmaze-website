Dyslexia is not a sentence, but a different way of processing information. Your child can be very talented and successful. The key is to notice difficulties early and find the right approach.

The first step is to consult a specialist (speech therapist or learning specialist) for diagnosis. After that, an individual training program can be created.

At home, you can help your child by reading together every day, using multisensory methods (letters from clay, sand drawing), and always praising any progress.
