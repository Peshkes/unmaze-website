### Myth 1: Dyslexia is a sign of low intelligence

Reality: dyslexia has nothing to do with IQ. Many famous people with dyslexia have achieved outstanding success.

### Myth 2: The child is just lazy

Reality: children with dyslexia put in more effort than their peers; their brain simply processes text differently.

### Myth 3: Dyslexia will go away with age

Reality: without specialist help, difficulties only accumulate.
